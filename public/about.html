About Ghost Mode

The growth-window method is our answer to a simple observation: the best trends are invisible at first.

In 2023 we tracked 400 trends from first sight to peak. 73% of those that crossed 1M posts spent at least 4 days under 20k posts. That is the window. Most tools ignore it because there is no API for small data. You have to watch manually.

We started Ghost Mode with three rules:

Rule 1: Under 20k or it doesn't exist. If a format has more than 20,000 posts, it is too late for early advantage. We archive it and move on.

Rule 2: Three independent origins. A trend is real only if three unrelated creators make it without copying. One viral video is not a trend; it's an outlier.

Rule 3: Save > Like. We prioritize comment-to-save ratio over likes. Saves indicate intent to recreate. Likes indicate passive approval. Ghost Mode hunts for recreation intent.

We built throwable beings to make research tactile. You throw a trend into the lab, you commit to testing it. That physical metaphor matters on mobile — it turns consumption into action.
