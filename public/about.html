About Ghost Mode

Ghost Mode is an internet trend discovery lab. It brings together public signals from search, developer communities, social conversations, and news so people can investigate what is gaining attention.

What you can do

  • Scan emerging topics in The Pulse.
  • Compare the public sources behind a signal.
  • Explore early or single-source topics in The Underground.
  • Save signals and choose interests in your local browser.

How to read a Ghost Score

Ghost Score is a directional index built from freshness, available engagement, source overlap, and rank. It is not a probability that a topic will go viral. Source coverage is incomplete and topic clustering is heuristic, so inspect the linked original sources.

Data limitations

Ghost Mode uses public feeds and endpoints. It does not have privileged access to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, or private platform analytics. A topic appearing here is a research lead, not a guarantee of future reach.
