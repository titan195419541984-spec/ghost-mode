Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Ghost Mode is a public-source internet trend discovery service hosted at ghost-mode-umm5.onrender.com.

Information and storage

This release does not require an account. Saved signals and topic preferences are stored in your browser using localStorage. The service receives ordinary web request information needed to deliver pages and public trend feeds. No user profile database or analytics SDK is enabled in this release.

Public data and images

Trend and headline data comes from public third-party sources. When available, card photography is retrieved from Wikimedia Commons with source and license information. External links are governed by the destination provider's privacy practices.

Advertising

Advertising is currently disabled in this deployment. A HilltopAds verification meta tag is present, but verification metadata alone does not load advertising or collect ad interactions. Google AdSense publisher verification is configured, but ad serving remains disabled until approval and valid ad-unit configuration are in place. If advertising is enabled in a future release, the selected advertising provider and its partners may receive browser, device, network, and interaction information and may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

Your choices

Clear this site's local storage and site data in your browser to remove saved signals and preferences. For privacy questions or requests, email ghostmode.trends@gmail.com. Ghost Mode is not directed to children under 13.
